Common Questions

Visit pmkisan.gov.in and click "New Farmer Registration" under Farmers Corner. Enter your Aadhaar number and state. Fill in your personal details, land records, and bank account information. Alternatively, visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with your Aadhaar, land records, and bank passbook. Helpline: 155261.

Visit pmjay.gov.in and click "Am I Eligible?" option. Enter your mobile number and OTP to check. You can also call the helpline 14555 or 1800-111-565. Your eligibility is based on the SECC 2011 database for rural areas and defined occupational criteria for urban areas.

Most government schemes require: (1) Aadhaar Card – most important, (2) Active Bank Account linked to Aadhaar, (3) Mobile number linked to Aadhaar, (4) Income Certificate from tehsildar/SDM, (5) Ration Card for BPL schemes, (6) Caste Certificate for SC/ST/OBC schemes, (7) Residence/Domicile Certificate. Specific schemes may require additional documents.

Common Service Centres (CSCs) are government-authorized service centres available at gram panchayat level across India. They help citizens access digital government services, apply for schemes, and complete Aadhaar-based transactions. Find nearest CSC at locator.csccloud.in. Services at CSCs are heavily subsidized.
