About BenefitBridge

Making government benefits accessible to every Indian citizen — without middlemen, confusion, or discrimination

Our Mission

Every eligible Indian citizen deserves direct access to government benefits

🚫

No Middlemen

We eliminate brokers and agents who exploit citizens by charging fees for free government services. All links go directly to official government portals.

🔍

Full Transparency

Clear information about eligibility criteria, required documents, and exact benefits for every scheme. No hidden conditions or misleading information.

🌐

Accessible to All

Voice-powered assistance in Hindi and English. Simple language. Mobile-first design. Works on basic smartphones with limited connectivity.

How the Platform Works

1️⃣

Data from Official Sources

Our scheme database is built from official government websites, ministry press releases, and government gazette notifications. We maintain accuracy and update regularly.

2️⃣

Client-Side Processing

All eligibility calculations happen in your browser. We never collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Your privacy is completely protected.

3️⃣

Direct to Official Portals

Every scheme has a direct link to its official government website. We never create our own application forms. Citizens always apply through official channels.

4️⃣

AI Voice Assistant

Our AI voice assistant uses the Web Speech API to understand queries in Hindi and English, providing instant answers about schemes, eligibility, and processes without any external API calls.

Our Key Principles

🆓

Always Free

BenefitBridge will always be completely free to use. We do not charge any fees, commissions, or subscriptions.

🔒

Privacy First

No data collection, no cookies, no tracking. All processing is client-side. We believe your information belongs to you.

✅

Accuracy

We regularly verify scheme information against official sources. Outdated information is promptly corrected.

🤝

Inclusive

Built for every Indian — from farmers in rural Uttar Pradesh to entrepreneurs in urban Mumbai. Language barriers addressed through voice assistance.
